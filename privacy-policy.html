PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 2026 | Effective for Manjutha Batteries & Manjunath Enterprises

1. OVERVIEW & LEGAL IDENTITY

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which Manjunath Enterprises (operating under the trade name Manjutha Batteries) collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from visitors of this website and users contacting us for automotive battery assistance and sales in Bengaluru, Karnataka.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect personal information solely to fulfill your service requests, including:

  • Contact Details: Your mobile phone number, name, and email address (if provided).
  • Location Data: Your physical breakdown location, neighborhood, or GPS location shared via WhatsApp or phone call.
  • Vehicle Details: Car make, model, and battery issue described to ensure compatibility.
  • Technical Usage Data: Anonymized interactions via Google Tag Manager to evaluate landing page performance.

3. HOW WE USE COLLECTED INFORMATION

We utilize the collected information strictly for legitimate service fulfillment:

  • To contact you directly regarding your battery jump-start or replacement request.
  • To dispatch a mobile service technician to your vehicle's location.
  • To register official manufacturer warranty cards (for Amaron, Exide units) upon installation.

4. ZERO DATA SALE GUARANTEE

We do NOT sell, trade, rent, or commercialize your personal information to third parties. Your phone number and location are only shared internally with the assigned technician handling your request.

5. COOKIES & TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES

Our website uses standard browser cookies and Google Tag Manager (GTM) to evaluate conversion metrics (such as phone call button taps and form submissions). These cookies do not store financial or sensitive personal credentials.

6. DATA RETENTION & SECURITY

We retain lead form submissions only as long as necessary to fulfill service, accounting, and manufacturer warranty obligations. We employ encrypted HTTPS protocols across all pages.

7. YOUR RIGHTS & DATA DELETION REQUESTS

You have the right to request access to the information we hold about you or request its immediate deletion from our records. To request removal of your contact details, simply contact us at the address below.
