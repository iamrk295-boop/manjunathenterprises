PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 2026 | Effective for Manjunath Enterprises & JumpStart Battery Experts

1. OVERVIEW & LEGAL IDENTITY

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which Manjunath Enterprises (operating under the trade name JumpStart Battery Experts) collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from visitors of this website (manjunathent.in) and users contacting us for emergency roadside battery assistance and battery sales in Bengaluru, Karnataka.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect personal information solely to fulfill your service requests, including:

  • Contact Details: Your name, phone number, and email address (if provided).
  • Location Data: Your physical breakdown location, neighborhood, or live GPS pin shared via WhatsApp or phone.
  • Vehicle Details: Car make, model, year, and battery specifications required to bring the correct replacement unit.
  • Technical Usage Data: Anonymized browser type, referring URL, time of visit, and interactions via Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics.

3. HOW WE USE COLLECTED INFORMATION

We utilize the collected information strictly for legitimate business operations:

  • To immediately dispatch our closest mobile service technician to your vehicle.
  • To communicate ETA, pricing estimates, and status updates regarding your battery assistance request.
  • To process invoicing, battery warranty registration with manufacturers (Amaron, Exide, Livguard), and scrap buyback rebates.
  • To monitor and improve the efficiency of our website and Google Ads campaign performance.

4. ZERO DATA SALE GUARANTEE

We do NOT sell, trade, rent, or commercialize your personal information to third parties. Your phone number and location are only shared internally with the assigned technician dispatched to your vehicle.

5. COOKIES & TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES

Our website uses standard browser cookies and Google Tag Manager (GTM) to evaluate conversion metrics (such as phone call button taps and form submissions). These cookies do not store identifiable personal banking credentials or passwords. You can configure your browser to reject cookies if you prefer.

6. DATA RETENTION & SECURITY

We retain lead form submissions only as long as necessary to provide service, fulfill accounting requirements, and honor manufacturer warranty registrations. We employ encrypted HTTPS communication protocols to safeguard all submitted data.

7. YOUR RIGHTS & DATA DELETION REQUESTS

Under applicable data protection guidelines, you have the right to request access to the information we hold about you or request its immediate deletion from our records. To request removal of your contact details, simply call us or contact us at the address below.
